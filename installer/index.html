Install Pocket Tank

A tiny language model keeps a fish tank alive on an ESP32-S3. Put it on your board from this page: no toolchain, no terminal, one button.

Install

  1. Plug the board into this computer with a USB-C cable that carries data (some charge-only cables don't). The board's own USB-C port goes straight to the chip, no adapter needed.
  2. Click the button. Your browser asks which port to use: pick USB JTAG/serial debug unit (on Linux it may just say ttyACM0).
  3. Wait for the bar to fill. About {{TOTAL_MB}} MB goes over, which takes a minute or two. When it says Installation complete, the board reboots into the tank on its own, with two fry waiting for you.
This browser can't talk to USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop or laptop. Browser flashing only works on an HTTPS page (or localhost). Chrome or Edge, desktop · firmware {{VERSION}}

The dialog offers to erase the device first. Say yes for a brand-new tank. If you're updating a tank you already keep, leave it off and your fish, their trust and their history stay exactly as they were.

Supported hardware

Supported

Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.8 (revision V1 and V2; the firmware tells them apart at boot). ESP32-S3 with 16 MB flash and 8 MB PSRAM, 1.8" 368×448 AMOLED, capacitive touch, IMU, RTC, battery charger. Around $35 with the screen and a battery.

The installer checks that the connected chip is an ESP32-S3. It can't tell which board the chip sits on, so on any other ESP32-S3 board it will flash fine and then show nothing: the display and touch drivers are for this panel. More boards later.

If something's off

The port list is empty, or the board isn't in it

A tank that is already running Pocket Tank may be asleep (dark screen): its USB port disappears while it drowses. Press BOOT once to wake it, then try again.

Otherwise, hold BOOT while you plug the cable in (or while tapping RESET). That forces the chip into its download mode, which always shows up as a port.

Try another cable and a port directly on the computer rather than a hub.

"Failed to initialize" or the install stops partway

Close anything else that may hold the port (a serial monitor, another tab with this page), unplug, replug, and click Install again. If it fails at the same place twice, hold BOOT while plugging in and retry with erase on.

Linux: permission denied on the port

Add yourself to the group that owns /dev/ttyACM0 (usually dialout or uucp), then log out and back in: sudo usermod -aG dialout $USER

Windows: the port shows but the install fails to connect

Windows 10 and 11 have the driver built in. If the device appears with a warning in Device Manager, install Espressif's USB driver or use a different USB port, then retry.

What exactly gets written

Four pieces at fixed places in the chip's 16 MB flash: the bootloader, the partition table, the app, and the 7.5 MB four-bit model. The manifest.json next to this page lists them. Everything runs on the chip; nothing is sent anywhere.

After it boots

Two fry appear in a fresh tank. Tap the water to feed them, rest a finger on the glass and they'll come to see you, drag across the glass to wipe algae, swipe sideways through the grass to trim it. A short press on BOOT puts the tank to sleep; hold it to power off; hold it and tap the glass to reset the tank.
